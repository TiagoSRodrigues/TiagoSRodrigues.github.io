Tips for Using These Exercises

  • Don't overthink it: Set a timer for 15-30 minutes and just write
  • Adapt freely: Modify any prompt to fit your interests or current project
  • Mix and match: Combine elements from different prompts
  • Return to favorites: If a prompt sparks something, explore it further
  • No pressure: Not every exercise needs to become a finished piece
  • Experiment with forms: Try poems, flash fiction, scenes, letters, etc.
  • Build on characters: Use the same character across multiple exercises
  • Share if you want: Join writing communities to share your work
  • Keep writing: Consistency matters more than perfection

Remember: The goal is to keep your creative muscles active and discover new directions for your writing. Some exercises will resonate more than others, and that's perfectly fine. Let your imagination lead the way!
